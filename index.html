MantisCore is the operating system powering a collection of applications, creative tools, simulations and experiences built for the M5Stack Core2.

The Operating System · M5Stack Core2

MantisCore

The home screen and application launcher for your Mantis device. Launch applications from SD card and preserve application progress and personal settings.

Applications

Space Exploration↗

SpaceMantis

Explore a vast spacefaring universe with ships, planetary worlds, trading, combat and multiplayer.
World Exploration↗

MantisPlanet

Explore procedural landscapes with forests, lakes, weather, wildlife and immersive sound. PLUS fishing and bug collecting!!
Driving Simulation↗

Mantis Rally

Procedural rally and off-road, branching roads, changing environments and physics-based driving.
Underwater Exploration↗

Mantis Seadiver

Explore underwater environments, reefs, kelp forests, wildlife and mysterious ocean depths.
Flight Simulation↗

Mantis Air

A flight simulator running on the same engine as Rally and Planet. Take jobs and advance to unlock new aircraft and craeer opportunities.
tap for new world
Interactive Visuals↗

Mantis Synapse

Audio-reactive psychedelic visuals and interactive modes controlled by touch, tilt and motion. Praying mantis themed of course. lots of minigames.
Camera System↗

MantisCam

Live camera viewing, streaming and photo, video and audio capture. deploy and manage cameras running my custom esp32cam firmware.
Media↗

Mantis Gallery

Image gallery for displsying the magic image format .mg files created by the mantis apps that have a camera mode, shuffle and slideshow functionality as well as parrallax and some physics simulations.
Music Production↗

Mantis Studio

A pocket music workstation with drum sequencing, synthesizers, recording and mixing. A dancing mantis awaits as well.
Artificial Intelligence↗

MantisKAI

Local LLM frontend and companion Tkinter server. Our mantiskai server talks to lmstudio to host local chat model and comfyui to generate images. custom profiles and voice command tools. streaming voice. all local and offline.
Communication↗

MantisChat

ESP-NOW group messaging, direct messages and shared whiteboard. Burnsble callsigns.
Wireless Tools↗

DeviceRadar

Offline Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE signal discovery and directional radar plus threat audit and channel state information motion detection using passive wifi snifffing.
Engineering Tools↗

MantisCalc

An engineering and trades calculator multitool.
